How it works

From slide to answer in one keystroke.

Works on PDFs, Google Slides and your university's lecture site, in any Chrome tab.

  1. Open a slide

    Any lecture in a Chrome tab. Open the side panel from the toolbar.

  2. Press Capture

    Or Alt+Shift+S. VisionLearn reads the slide only when you ask.

  3. Ask anything

    Answers stick to what's on screen and remember earlier slides from the same lecture.

  4. Study from it

    Slides are filed by topic. Each topic can quiz you and write you a study guide.

What it reads

The hard parts of a STEM slide, read properly.

Not a blob of OCR text. Each part of the slide comes back as what it is.

∇ · E = ρε₀"The field spreads out from charge."

Equations

Typeset properly, with a plain-language reading beside each one.

Diagrams and graphs

Nodes, edges and weights picked out, so you can ask about one figure.
