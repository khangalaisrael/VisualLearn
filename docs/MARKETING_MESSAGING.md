# VisionLearn AI — Marketing Messaging Kit

Ready-to-use words for pitching the VisionLearn AI slide-capture Chrome extension: taglines, pitches of different lengths, store copy, social posts, outreach scripts, and answers to objections.

> **Honesty rule:** Lead with what ships today, and label everything else as "coming soon". Shipping today: one-click slide capture; AI reading of text, equations, tables, charts, and diagrams; equations typeset as real math; graph-diagram structure extraction (nodes, edges, weights); and a chat that streams answers grounded in the captured slide. Coming soon: asking about a single figure, searching a whole deck, and the Concepts, Notes, and Quiz tabs.

## 1. Positioning

**One sentence:**
VisionLearn AI is a Chrome extension that reads your lecture slides the way a tutor would, including the equations, diagrams, and charts, so you can ask about exactly what's on screen and get an answer right away.

**Who it's for:** Students in computer science, AI/ML, engineering, and other STEM subjects. Also lecturers and researchers who work through slide-heavy material.

**The problem:**
STEM slides are dense with things a normal AI chat can't see: equations, graphs, circuit and network diagrams, tables. Students end up retyping formulas, describing diagrams in words, or taking screenshots and hoping the chatbot reads them correctly. The question usually comes up mid-lecture or mid-study, and by the time it's answered, the moment has passed.

**The promise:**
Capture the slide and ask your question. VisionLearn already understands what's on it.

**Why it's different:**
| Generic AI chat | VisionLearn AI |
|---|---|
| You copy, paste, or describe the slide | One click captures the slide you're looking at |
| Treats the slide as one flat image | Breaks it into objects: text, equations, tables, charts, diagrams |
| Shows equations as raw LaTeX gibberish | Displays equations as clean, typeset math |
| Guesses at diagram connections | Extracts graph structure (nodes, edges, weights, direction) from algorithm diagrams |
| Answers from general knowledge | Answers grounded in *your* slide |
| A separate tab and app | Sits in Chrome's side panel next to your lecture |

## 11. Words to use / avoid

**Use:** understand, explain, grounded, capture, your slide, equations, diagrams, study companion, tutor, one click, side panel.

**Avoid:**
- **"Never wrong" / "100% accurate":** it's AI, so promise help, not perfection.
- **"Does your homework":** it invites cheating concerns and the wrong users.
- **"Works on everything":** photos and curved hand-drawn diagrams are best-effort.
- **Advertising quizzes, notes, whole-deck search, or figure selection as live:** say "coming soon" until they ship.
- **"Better than ChatGPT":** say *what* is different (it captures, structures, and stays grounded in the slide) rather than making a claim you can't back up.
